Track My Ferry

Live NYC Ferry tracker that reads the boats' AIS signals, works out which scheduled trip each boat is running, and predicts arrivals as a time range along with the chance the boat is full. Numbers below are from three weeks of simulated service.

  • Streams live AIS positions, NYC Ferry's GTFS-realtime feed and NOAA tide data through Kafka, and matches each boat to its scheduled trip with 99.4% accuracy.
  • Predicts arrivals with gradient-boosted quantile models and conformal calibration: 48 s average error vs 92 s for a schedule-plus-delay baseline, with time ranges that contain the real arrival 81% of the time.
  • Serves a live city map over FastAPI WebSockets and React + MapLibre, backed by PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB, Docker and automated tests in CI.
